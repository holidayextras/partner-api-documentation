# Why Partner API 2026

Partner API 2026 is a single integration that gives you access to Holiday Extras' full inventory across UK and European markets. One authentication flow, one consistent API shape, and a growing catalogue of products: airport parking is available now, with transfers, lounges, and more already in the pipeline.

---

## Scale and reach

Holiday Extras is one of the UK and Europe's largest travel ancillary providers, with parking across 130 airports in 17 countries and growing. Every product is pre-vetted, with real availability and Price Lock support for checkout.

**Markets and currencies:** UK and European markets are accessible from a single integration, with prices in GBP and EUR.

**Languages:** Product content is localised into eight European languages: English, German, Dutch, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, and Polish. Your customers get information in their own language without you managing any translations.

**A growing catalogue:** Airport parking is available now. Transfers, lounges, and Fast Track are already in progress. Because the core integration patterns carry across every product - authentication, booking lifecycle, error handling - expanding your offering is an extension, not a restart.

---

## Built to integrate once

Every product in Partner API 2026 follows the same shape: same booking lifecycle, same response format, same error contract. Learn the pattern once and it applies across everything you add.

**Familiar standards throughout:**

- **OAuth 2.0** authentication: the standard your platform already speaks. One token covers all requests for an hour.
- **OpenAPI contracts** for every endpoint: generate client code, validate against the schema, and stay in sync as the API evolves.
- **IATA airport codes** for search: use the standard codes your platform already works with.
- **Structured, actionable error responses** with a trace ID for fast debugging and clear support escalations.
- **Idempotent booking flows:** duplicate bookings are prevented by design.
- **Sandbox environment:** build and break things without touching production.
- **Webhooks** for async events: no polling required. Your customers get information as soon as it is ready.

**Simpler booking flows:**

Token-based flows for product selection, amendment, and cancellation carry the details needed to complete the next step. The selected product, dates, currency, and quoted price are captured in the product token. Cancellation and refund policies are returned in search results, so customers see the terms before they commit.

**Price Lock:** the price shown in search is protected for a clear window. Holiday Extras absorbs eligible price increases during that time, giving you greater price certainty while the customer completes checkout. You do not need to repeatedly check the price, which keeps the integration simpler and reduces avoidable interruptions before booking.

## HX handles everything after the booking

Once a booking is made, Holiday Extras takes on the work of keeping your customer informed and the booking fulfilled. You decide how much of that you want to own.

**Hosted confirmation page:** every booking response includes a link to a Holiday Extras-hosted confirmation page. It contains everything the customer needs: entry code, directions, arrival and departure procedures. It stays up to date automatically as the supplier finalises details. For many partners, a link in the purchase confirmation email is all that is needed.

**Fulfilment and supplier management:** Holiday Extras manages the relationship with the supplier. If a supplier issue arises before or during the customer's trip, we go above and beyond to rebook them and make sure they have everything they need. Your customers' plans stay in place without you having to act.

**Consistent policies across all supply:** cancellation and refund policies are returned in a standardised format across all products and markets. You know exactly what to show your customers, and they can make an informed decision before they book.
